Inércia Demoparty 2006 - Competitions

All works submitted to the Inércia Demoparty competitions must be original works created entirely by their authors, or properly credited, under penalty of disqualification from the competition.

The organization reserves the right to disqualify from the competition any entry that is in legal or moral breach.

Demo - demo competition

A demo is a multimedia demonstration of synchronised sound and image, generally calculated in real time in its entirety. Due to the complexity of producing a demo, these are usually made by groups. In this competition demos will have a time limit of 10 minutes and may not exceed 20 Mb (20,971,520 bytes) of disk space. They will be shown on high-end PCs or on other hardware (consoles, computers) if necessary.

64k - 64k intro competition

A 64k intro is in every way similar to a demo, but with the extra difficulty of a very small disk space limit. Here too it is usually groups that create this kind of entry. Intros likewise have a limit of 8 minutes and a disk space limit of 64 Kb (65536 bytes).

4k - 4k intro competition

A 4k intro is the same as the 64k ones, but with an extreme size limit (4096 bytes). Many of these demonstrations serve only to show the efficiency of algorithms.

Wild - video and animation competition

Wildcompos are animations, or even video sequences, generated in the various programs available for that purpose, with total freedom given to their creator. Entries in this category must have a maximum size of 100Mb (104,857,600 bytes) and a maximum running time of 8 minutes.

mp3 - professional music competition

Professional music is understood to be any kind of music aimed at the general public. In this category the participant is free to choose any kind of instrument and/or digital music program. The work must however be delivered in digital form (in mp3, with a bitrate of at least 160kbps or VBR, or in ogg format), with a maximum time limit of 5 minutes. If the music exceeds that limit it will be faded out when presented to the audience at the event.

mmul - tracker music competition

Multi-channel music is made in programs created by sceners and is used many times over in demos or intros. In this category there will be a limit of 1 Mb (1,048,576 bytes) and 5 minutes of duration. If the music exceeds that limit it will be faded out when presented to the audience at the event. The mod, s3m, it, xm and rns formats are accepted. Entries using dx/vst effects are not accepted.

fastmusic - fast music competition

Made according to a theme and a time limit given during the party. The only restrictions in this competition are that external sources may not be used and that the work must be finished within the one hour time limit.

gfx 2d - two-dimensional graphics competition

A graphics competition where the participant is free to use any 2d graphics production program to create their work. When the work is shown, the programs used will be indicated. The participant must also submit several 'stages' of their work, in order to confirm that it is an original entry, and the methods used. In this competition there is a resolution limit of 800x600 pixels and works must be submitted in jpg or png format.

gfx 3d - three-dimensional graphics competition

A graphics competition where the participant is free to use any 3d graphics production program to create their work. When the work is shown, the programs used will be indicated. The participant must also submit several 'stages' of their work, in order to confirm that it is an original entry, and the methods used. In this competition there is a resolution limit of 800x600 pixels and works must be submitted in jpg or png format.

fast gfx - fast graphics competition

Fast gfx (fast graphics) are images made according to a theme and a time limit given during the party. The only restrictions in this competition are that external sources may not be used and that the work must be finished within the one hour time limit.

pirated - pirated demo competition

The goal is to alter the data files of a previously released demo in order to create a different demo.
